# 🏆 Task 4 — Final Team Challenge

🎯 Goal
Work in a team to apply everything you’ve learned throughout the session:

- Branching
- Opening Pull Requests (PRs)
- Reviewing and merging
- Resolving merge conflicts

## 🔧 What You'll Do

- Each member creates a feature branch and adds their section to `TEAM.md`.
- Open Pull Requests and perform reviews for teammates.
- Experience and resolve at least one merge conflict together.
- Merge all approved contributions into `main`.

---

## 🧠 Collaboration Rules

- Use meaningful branch names and commit messages.
- Every PR must be reviewed and approved by another teammate before merging.
- Communicate as a team when resolving merge conflicts.
- Keep `main` clean and functional at all times.

---

## 🏁 Win Conditions

- Every team member has one merged PR.
- At least one merge conflict is successfully resolved.
- The final `TEAM.md` includes all team members’ sections.
- All feature branches are merged and deleted after completion.

---

🎉 When your team meets these goals, you’ve officially “shipped” your release and completed the Git Quest!
